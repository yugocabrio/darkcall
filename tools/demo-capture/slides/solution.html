How it works

One sealed auction. Three cryptographic layers.

01 · REP3

Split

Each bid becomes secret shares for three operators. No single operator can read one.

02 · coNoir

Prove

The operators compute the clearing price on the shares and prove it together in zero knowledge.

03 · Halo2

Pay

One shielded Zcash transaction. Each payout’s amount and recipient is proven to match the result.

Settled on public Zcash testnet · every payout proven · signed 2-of-3 with FROST

DarkCall answers with three layers. REP3 splits each bid into secret shares, so no single operator can read it. coNoir lets the three operators compute the clearing price on those shares and prove it together, so no prover ever holds the bid book. Halo2 proves that each payout of the one shielded Zcash transaction matches the result. It has settled on public Zcash testnet, signed two-of-three with FROST.
