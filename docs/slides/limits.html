Trust model

What DarkCall does not claim

Semi-honest MPC. Secrecy holds against one corrupt operator; two colluding operators can rebuild the bids.

Operators see outcomes. They learn who won and each refund, never a bid.

Custody is 2-of-3. Two colluding signers can move the escrow; that payout fails public verification.

The book is signed, not proven complete. A dropped bid is visible to its bidder, not to outsiders.

One host in the demo. Every role runs on one machine, and one test wallet funds all four tickets.

Prototype scale. Four bids per auction, testnet coins only, no audit.

We state the limits as plainly as the results. Bid secrecy assumes at most one semi-honest operator; two colluding operators could rebuild the bids. Operators learn who won and each refund, but never a bid. Custody is 2-of-3, so two colluding signers could move the escrow, though such a payout fails public verification. The accepted book is signed rather than proven complete, so a dropped bid is visible to its bidder but not to outsiders. And this is a prototype: every role runs on one host in the demo, auctions have four bids, and only testnet coins are used.
