An auction runs in four stages. Each bidder’s own client commits to its bid, splits it into three secret shares and proves that they hold the same bid. Bidders then lock fixed-size shielded deposits into a 2-of-3 escrow, and all three operators sign the accepted book. The operators clear the auction on the shares and prove the price together, without rebuilding any bid. Finally the six payout bindings are checked and any two operators sign one shielded transaction. Anyone can verify it with no secrets, and each bidder decrypts only its own receipt.
