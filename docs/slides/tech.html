Here is exactly what is proven. Each bid is split into three field elements that sum to it, and every share is held by two operators. The client’s proof ties the public commitment to the share pair each operator receives. The operators then compute the witness on shares, and coNoir turns it into one UltraHonk proof whose public statement fixes the terms, the commitments, the signed accepted root and the receivers, and outputs the price, the units sold and six row hashes. For each payout, one Halo2 proof on Pallas recomputes that row hash in BN254 arithmetic and opens the note commitment in the Zcash transaction, which ties the two curves together.
