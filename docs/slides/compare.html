How does this compare? Commit–reveal and viewing-key audits let anyone check the price, but only by opening every bid. Designs that decrypt after the close and then prove the result keep bids from the public, but whoever decrypts reads them all. A trusted auctioneer asks you to trust both the price and the payouts. DarkCall is the column where the bids are hidden even from the people running the auction, the price is checked by a proof, and every payout is proven to match.
