Only the clearing price and the number of units sold are public. No bid, no winner’s maximum, and not even the demand at any other price: there is no demand curve to read. Who won, each refund and which bidder paid which deposit stay private. One honest caveat: by the auction rule the price equals the highest losing bid or the reserve, so that one amount follows from the public price.
