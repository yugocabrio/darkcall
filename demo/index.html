About this demo +
Sealing a bid gives instant signed pre-checks of its proof and shares. Fund on Zcash then makes a real deposit for every sealed ticket on an isolated NU7 regtest (budget +15,000 zatoshi each, from one local test wallet). The operators admit the deposits under a chain-funded certificate over the same accepted root. Only then does REP3 clear the book, and Settle on Zcash pays out in one shielded transaction. The local helper sees typed demo bids and creates their proofs/shares on this PC.
Four tickets are required. After the actual cutoff, three operators sign the accepted book and run the same secret shares through REP3. The fixed proof and signed root are verified before your ticket pads are opened. One host, semi-honest MPC; privacy assumes at most one corrupt party.
The opening capability stays in page memory; reload loses access. This is not production authentication, private browser proving or native memo delivery.

Trust model & limitations +
  • Bid secrecy assumes at most one semi-honest REP3 party is corrupt. Two colluding parties can reconstruct bids.
  • Custody is 2-of-3. Two signers can move funds; users cannot force a unilateral refund.
  • Operators have escrow viewing capability and learn refunds and winning status. Public outcomes can allow inference.
  • One-host prototype on isolated regtest. No public-testnet, malicious-MPC or production-custody claim.
  • Accepted-book signatures attest funding; they are not a public ZK funding proof. The configured admission authority is trusted separately.
  • Item delivery, network anonymity and Sybil resistance are outside this prototype.
No chain inclusion proof in the public bundle.
Recorded settlement; separate offline rehearsal with synthetic funding.
